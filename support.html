Common questions

I forgot my password.
On the login screen tap "Forgot password?", enter your email, and use the code we send you.

I never got my confirmation email.
Check your spam folder. On the login screen, try to log in and tap "Resend email" when prompted.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the app, open Profile, then Settings and appearance, and tap "Delete my account." This permanently deletes your profile, content, matches and messages. You can also email us to ask.

How do I report someone or something?
Use "Report" on any post, comment, message, listing or profile. You can also "Block" a person to stop seeing each other. For urgent safety concerns, email us with details.

Someone is asking me for money or acting suspicious.
Do not send money. Report them in the app and email us.

How do I remove myself from the map?
Go to Discover, tap Map, then "My pin," and choose "Remove my pin."
