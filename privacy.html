FadeSpace Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

FadeSpace ("we", "us") is operated by Fade Space. FadeSpace helps barbers and barbershops connect, rent chairs, share their work, and buy and sell gear. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: tysonjorge0123@icloud.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored scrambled by our authentication provider; we cannot read it), and whether you are a barber or a shop.
  • Profile and portfolio: name or shop name, headline, tagline, city, bio, profile photo, banner, years of experience, hourly rate, specialties or amenities, what you are looking for, links you choose to add (such as Instagram or a booking page), portfolio and shop photos and videos, and an optional PDF resume.
  • Shop details: your chair listing (title, description, pricing, photos) and private contact details (contact person, phone, street address).
  • Activity: your likes and passes, matches, rental requests, reviews and ratings, posts, comments and likes, marketplace listings, team invitations and memberships, and the reports and blocks you make.
  • Messages: messages and photos you send in chats with matches and with marketplace buyers and sellers.
  • Location: only if you choose to appear on the map. You can enter a city or address, or share your current location while using the app. We blur it before anyone else can see it: shops appear within roughly 100 meters and barbers within roughly one mile. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Availability schedule: the days and hours barbers choose to appear as available, and your device's time zone so notifications arrive at the right time.
  • Device and notifications: a push notification token if you allow notifications. Your theme and layout choices are stored on your device.
  • Taps on recommended products: when you tap an item in FadeSpace Picks, we record the tap with your account so we can count it once per person.

We do not collect your contacts. FadeSpace does not show third-party ads and does not use advertising trackers. We ask for photo library access only so you can choose pictures, videos and documents to upload.

How we use it

We use your information to run FadeSpace: creating and securing your account, showing your profile to others, creating matches, delivering messages and notifications, showing the map and availability, displaying ratings and reviews, keeping the community safe (including filtering and reviewing reports), counting product taps, and answering your questions. We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see what

  • Your name, headline, city, bio, photos, portfolio, links, chair listing, team membership, posts, ratings and reviews can be seen by other signed-in FadeSpace users. Reviews show the reviewer's name.
  • A shop's contact person, phone number and street address are visible only to barbers the shop has matched with.
  • Your resume can be opened only by you and by shops.
  • A barber's map pin is visible only to shops, only while the barber is available. A shop's pin is visible to signed-in users while it has an active chair listing.
  • Messages and chat photos are visible only to the people in that conversation.
  • Suspended or blocked accounts are hidden from the people involved.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host our database, authentication and file storage in the United States; Expo, Apple and Google to deliver push notifications; an email delivery provider to send account emails such as confirmations and password codes; and Apple Maps to display the map on iPhones. These providers process data on our behalf. When you tap a news story or a product link, you leave FadeSpace and the other site's own policies apply. Some product links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy.

Payments

FadeSpace does not currently process payments. Marketplace deals and chair rentals are arranged directly between users. If we add payments or subscriptions, we will use a payment processor or the App Store, and we will update this policy first.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active. Content you delete, such as a post or listing, is removed. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile, then Settings and appearance, then Delete my account). This permanently deletes your profile, photos, videos, resume, listings, posts, comments, matches, messages, team links, availability schedule and reviews. We may keep reports about conduct for safety and legal reasons, and copies in backups may remain for a limited time.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, change who sees what through your profile settings, remove your map pin, turn off notifications in your phone settings, block or report users, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your information; email us and we will help.

Children

FadeSpace is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including access rules that limit who can read each type of data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
