Chairs Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Chairs ("we", "us") is operated by YOUR_NAME_OR_COMPANY. Chairs helps barbers and barbershops find each other and arrange chair rentals. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, your password (stored in scrambled form by our authentication provider; we cannot see it), and whether you signed up as a barber or a shop.
  • Profile information you add: your name or shop name, headline, city, bio, profile photo, and for barbers, years of experience, hourly rate, and portfolio photos and videos.
  • Shop information: chair listing details (title, description, pricing, photos) and private contact details (contact person, business phone, street address).
  • Activity in the app: likes and passes, matches, messages, rental requests, ratings and reviews you give or receive, and reports and blocks you submit.
  • Device information: a push notification token, if you allow notifications.

We do not collect your precise location. The "city" shown on your profile is whatever you type in. Chairs asks for photo library access only so you can choose pictures and videos to upload.

How we use it

We use your information to run the app: creating your account, showing your profile to other users, creating matches, delivering messages and notifications, displaying ratings and reviews, keeping the community safe, and responding to support requests. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

  • Your name or shop name, headline, city, bio, photos, portfolio, chair listing, ratings and reviews are visible to other signed-in Chairs users.
  • A shop's contact person, phone number, and street address are visible only to barbers the shop has matched with.
  • Messages and rental requests are visible only to the two people in the match.
  • Reviews show the reviewer's name next to the review.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host our database, authentication, and file storage, and Expo (with Apple and Google) to deliver push notifications. These providers process data on our behalf. In-app payments are not currently offered; if we add them, a payment processor such as Stripe will handle payment details and we will update this policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile, then "Delete my account"). This permanently deletes your profile, photos, portfolio, listing, matches, messages, rental requests, and reviews. We may keep reports about conduct for safety and legal reasons, and copies in backups may remain for a limited time.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app, turn off notifications in your phone settings, block or report other users, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or correct your information; contact us and we will help.

Children

Chairs is for adults. You must be at least 18 to use it, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
